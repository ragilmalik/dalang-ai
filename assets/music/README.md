# Music

The films use a real gamelan recording under the scenes:

| File | What it is | Credit and licence |
|---|---|---|
| `kraton-yogyakarta-1.ogg` | Javanese gamelan, court musicians of the Kraton Ngayogyakarta Hadiningrat, Yogyakarta, excerpt 1 (2:14). Calm and night scenes (and the misty, golden ones). | Recorded by RTB45 on 2014-11-06, [freesound.org/people/RTB45/sounds/255544](https://freesound.org/people/RTB45/sounds/255544/), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gamelan_angka_1_Kraton_Ngayogyakarta.ogg). | `kraton-yogyakarta-2.ogg` | Same ensemble, excerpt 2 (3:56). Tense, happy and triumphant scenes. | Recorded by RTB45, [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gamelan_angka_2_Kraton_Ngayogyakarta.ogg). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). |

Changes I make: the recordings are cut, looped, lowered in volume and ducked under the narration. They are not
endorsed by the musicians or the recordist.

`GAMELAN_SOURCE=procedural` switches back to the gamelan made in code (`api/pipeline/gamelan.py`), which needs no
audio files and no licence. The gong and bonang cues on the title and end cards are always made in code.
