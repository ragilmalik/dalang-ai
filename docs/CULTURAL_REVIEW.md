# Cultural review checklist (for a dalang or sanggar wayang)

Dalang AI shows children real wayang kulit tradition: the "Kocap kacarita" opening, the gunungan between scenes,
wayang archetypes and a short cultural note under every puppet. These are written by Muse Spark from the
prompts in `docs/prompts.md` and **have not yet been reviewed by a dalang or sanggar**. Please review one or more
finished films (watch page, "Di balik wayang · Behind the puppets") and the printable puppets PDF.

Reviewer: please tick each line, or write what should change.

## A. Opening and structure
- [ ] The opening line ("Kocap kacarita, ...") is used respectfully and correctly for a children's story.
- [ ] The gunungan (kayon) opening the film and sweeping between scenes is appropriate.
- [ ] "Gara-gara" is a fair name for the pause where the child chooses what happens next (in a real pagelaran
      it is the turning point where the punakawan appear). Suggested wording if not: ____________________
- [ ] The gamelan-style sound (synthesised gong and bonang pattern, slendro-ish notes) is acceptable for children.

## B. Puppets and archetypes
- [ ] Archetype labels (alus, gagah, punakawan, raksasa/buta, wayang animals) are used correctly.
- [ ] Puppet pictures (side profile, tatahan perforation, sunggingan colours) do not misrepresent a specific
      sacred or court figure.
- [ ] Punakawan (Semar, Gareng, Petruk, Bagong) are portrayed with appropriate character, if they appear.

## C. Cultural notes (one per puppet)
- [ ] Each note is true and kid-friendly.
- [ ] No note invents a sacred meaning or a ritual rule.
- [ ] Notes in Basa Jawa use a register suitable for children (ngoko alus or as you advise).
      Notes to correct (film id, puppet, suggested text): ____________________

## D. Printable puppets
- [ ] The "shadow play at home" instructions (bamboo skewer, torch, white sheet) are safe and sensible.
- [ ] Nothing in the printouts should not be cut or played with.

## E. Verdict
- [ ] I am comfortable with Dalang AI being shown to children as an introduction to wayang kulit.
- Changes I would like before public use: ____________________
